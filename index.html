Walk Grid

The step counter widget that shows your year. Every day, a square.

My grid is empty or missing old days

Walk Grid reads from Apple Health. Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Walk Grid and turn on Steps, Walking + Running Distance and Active Energy. History appears the next time you open the app.

How do I add or change a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Walk Grid. To change what a widget shows, touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget.

My widget isn't up to date

iOS limits how often widgets refresh, so they update through the day rather than every second. Opening the app refreshes them right away. While your iPhone is locked, Apple Health can't be read, so widgets show the last known count until you unlock.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings in the app (gear icon on Today) and tap Restore Purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.
