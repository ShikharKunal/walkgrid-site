The short version

Walk Grid is a step counter widget for iPhone. Walk Grid Pro is available as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple handles every payment. Walk Grid reads from Apple Health and keeps your data on your iPhone.

Walk Grid Pro

Walk Grid Pro unlocks the extra widgets, themes, stats and Trends. It is offered three ways, all of which unlock the same features and work with Family Sharing:

  • Yearly and Monthly are auto-renewing subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends. The subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone; cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Pro until the period you paid for ends. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.
  • Lifetime is a single payment with no renewal.

Restore any of them on another device with Restore on the Pro screen or Restore Purchases in Settings.

Purchases are made through Apple, and Apple's terms apply. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, not by the developer.

Health data, not medical advice

Steps, distance and active energy come from Apple Health and from your iPhone's sensors. These figures are estimates. Walk Grid is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing how much you exercise.
