Privacy Policy

Walk Grid · Effective 23 October 2026

The short version

Your steps and other health data are read from Apple Health and stay on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Walk Grid sends anonymous usage events (for example "finished onboarding" or "opened the Pro screen") so the developer can see which parts of the app work. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and you can turn usage events off in Settings.

Health data

With your permission, Walk Grid reads these types from Apple Health: step count, walking + running distance, active energy and exercise minutes. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • The data is used only to show your statistics, heatmap, milestones and widgets inside the app.
  • It is processed on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • It is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never shared with third parties.
  • You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Walk Grid.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps your settings (daily goal, units) and a small summary of recent days so its widgets can display them. This stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, email or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Anonymous usage events

Walk Grid uses PostHog (servers in the United States) to count how the app is used. What is sent:

  • Which onboarding screens were seen or skipped, whether Apple Health and notification access were allowed, the daily goal and unit you picked, whether the Pro screen was opened and whether a purchase started, succeeded or failed, and when the app is opened or closed.
  • The app version, iOS version, device model, language, and a random identifier created on your iPhone so events from one install can be counted together. It is not tied to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier.

What is never sent: step counts, distances, any Apple Health data, your location, or anything you type. PostHog receives your IP address when an event arrives and uses it only for an approximate country; it is set not to keep it.

The data is used only to improve the app. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not combined with data from other companies. To stop it, turn off Settings › Share anonymous usage in Walk Grid.

Third parties

Apart from PostHog above, the app contains no third-party SDKs, crash reporters or ad networks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Your health data lives in Apple Health, which you control. Usage events are anonymous and not linked to you, so the developer cannot look up which events came from you. To stop sending them, turn off Share anonymous usage in Settings, or email walkgrid.support@gmail.com with any question. This applies wherever you live, including under GDPR and CCPA.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: walkgrid.support@gmail.com (Shikhar Kunal).
